## What's new

- **Esri first, if you prefer it.** In **Settings › Pictures and download**, **Where the pictures come from** has a new choice: **Esri (ArcGIS), then Bing where Esri has no picture**. In some regions Esri's photos are newer or clearer than Bing's; Bing then fills the gaps. Bing first stays the recommended choice.
- **Check for updates.** A new **Check for updates** button at the top of the **Home** page looks for a new version straight away, without waiting for the regular check. When one is found, it is downloaded and offered as usual; otherwise OrthoStreamXP tells you that you already have the newest version, or why it could not look.

## Fixes

- A new version ready to install is now shown at the top of the **Home** page, above the status card, instead of further down where it was easy to miss.
- Sliders on the **Settings** page, and the values next to them, now look greyed out when they cannot be changed (while the scenery is streaming, or when their setting is off), like the switches already did.

## Good to know

- Your choice of where the pictures come from is not changed by the update. Like every setting, it can be changed only while OrthoStreamXP is stopped, and applies from the next start.
